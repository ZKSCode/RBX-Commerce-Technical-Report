## Project entry

**RBX Commerce | Full-stack web development**

Retail management system built with PHP and Laravel, covering variant-level inventory, product administration, point of sale, and cash reconciliation. The interface uses Blade, Alpine.js, and Tailwind CSS.

## Interview summary

> RBX Commerce combines inventory control with counter sales. Take a T-shirt with several colors and sizes. Staff can track each variant's stock and review discrepancies after a physical count. At the counter, the cashier finds the exact item by barcode, and the backend records the outgoing stock against that variant. At closing, the cashier counts the drawer and the system reconciles it against the opening balance and cash movements.
